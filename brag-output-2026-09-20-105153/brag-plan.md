# Brag Plan: GTM-360 Content Engine

## What is this app?
GTM-360 is "The Revenue Operating System" — six products (Compass, Cockpit, Crew, Method, Content Engine) all driven by one agent engine of 25 agents in 6 outcome groups (ORIENT · PLAN · CREATE · SELL · SUSTAIN · GOVERN). The Showcase hero makes the pitch: "One engine. Six outcomes." Content Engine is the flashiest of the six — a content engine that grounds every claim in real research, fact-checks before publish, writes in your voice, and ships to every channel.

## The angle
**One engine. Six outcomes.** The enterprise product-family reveal. Dark premium shader-gradient aesthetic, deep teal/emerald accents, calm confident pacing. The engine is the story: 25 agents arranged into 6 outcome groups running one operating loop. Specific, credible, enterprise-serious — restraint is the creative choice. No hype, no "AI magic" language; operators, not gurus.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark ink background, a slow-blooming emerald radial glow. The eyebrow line "The Revenue Operating System" fades in uppercase-spaced, then "One engine." slams in, followed by "Six outcomes." in the emerald→teal gradient — exactly the site's hero. Stats pulse in beneath: 6 products · 25 agents · 6 outcome groups.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The engine loop** — the six outcome groups appear one by one as bordered cards (ORIENT → PLAN → CREATE → SELL → SUSTAIN → GOVERN), each with its group color and a count of its agents, forming the operating loop. Beat-aligned sequential reveal.
- **Content Engine's proof** — grounded in real research (Tavily · Hacker News · Reddit · OpenAlex · Semantic Scholar · Wikipedia), fact-checked before publish, written in your voice. One claim per beat.
- **"Every channel, one draft"** — LinkedIn · YouTube · Substack · email sliding in as channel chips from the same checked core.

## Outro / punchline
Soft crossfade to the CTA band gradient (teal→ink). "Stop building a GTM machine by hand." → "GTM-360" wordmark + "The Revenue Operating System." Hold, then a clean fade.

## User flow worth showing
None — landing-page only (Showcase is the marketing surface; the app is behind auth). Rely on Key moments: the hero reveal, the engine loop, and the proof rows are the strongest visuals.

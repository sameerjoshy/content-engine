# Brag Plan: Content Engine — How the Pipeline Works

## What is this app?
GTM-360 Content Engine: an outcome-driven agent engine that turns a topic + angle into a citable, fact-checked article in your brand voice — then distributes it to LinkedIn, Substack, and X. Seven agents, one handoff each.

## The angle
**The assembly line.** A topic goes in one end, a fact-checked article comes out the other. Seven stations, each with one job and one output it hands to the next: the draft is a package that travels the track, collecting a stamp at every stage. No magic, no hype — the system assembling itself, claim by claim.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark ink, emerald glow. Eyebrow "THE CONTENT ENGINE" → "One topic in." then "A citable article out." in the brand gradient. A track with 8 numbered stations fades in beneath.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The package travels.** A glowing "THE DRAFT" pill slides station to station — the handoff made visible. Each arrival lights the station and swaps the stage card.
- **8 stages, 8 stamps (accurate to workflow.ts):**
  1. VALIDATOR — "Is this angle fresh?" → Go · Pivot · Stop
  2. RESEARCHER — "Find the proof" → Evidence table · claims, sourced
  3. SPEC BUILDER — "Set the brief" → Structure · register · proof mix
  4. SO-WHAT GATE — "Is it burning now?" → Demand score · pass or kill
  5. POV — "Ask the Experience Layer" → Proprietary POV · or "analytical"
  6. WRITER — "Write in your voice" → First draft
  7. EDITOR — "Check every claim" → Fact-checked · on-voice · scored
  8. SHIP — "Send it out" → LinkedIn · Substack · X

## Outro / punchline
Final card: "Fact-checked. On-voice. Every channel." → GTM-360 mark + "The Content Engine". One bell, then calm.

## User flow worth showing
The pipeline itself IS the flow — entry (topic) → action (7 stage handoffs) → result (citable article). Shown as the traveling package.
